DEEPscope Sun, planet and moon surface imagery
=============================================

These are reference images, independent of the simulation data and its units.
Their use does not imply endorsement by the source organisations. Image licences
below apply separately from the repository's software licence. Include these
credits when redistributing the images or figures containing them.

All viewer textures are 2:1 equirectangular maps, with north at the top.
Downloaded images are unchanged unless processing is explicitly described below.
The viewer uses +z north and places the map centre along +x at zero Texture
longitude. Positive Texture longitude shifts the imagery toward +y without
rotating the simulation, clipping planes or underlying fields. Map processing,
coverage and longitude conventions are source-dependent: verify alignment
against the source before using an image as a geographic measurement layer.

textures, not calibrated colour, elevation or albedo data. Venus is a coloured

references, without alignment to a simulation's time or longitude system.

RODINIA AT 1000 Ma -- rodinia-1000ma.png -- 2048 x 1024
Credit: DEEPscope rendering, after Z. X. Li and coauthors (2008); digital model
distributed by EarthByte. Source data and this rendering: CC BY 4.0.
Citation: Li, Z. X., et al. (2008). Assembly, configuration, and break-up history
of Rodinia: A synthesis. Precambrian Research 160, 179-210.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.precamres.2007.04.021
Model download:
https://www.earthbyte.org/webdav/ftp/Data_Collections/Li_etal_2008_RodiniaModel.zip
Downloaded archive SHA-256:
e659371df79acfd7e599d0a358be0b154705b84d92388c042e6382ef78a3f4f6
Licence notice is included as License.txt inside the downloaded archive, also at:
https://www.earthbyte.org/webdav/ftp/Data_Collections/License.txt
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The journal paper retains its publisher copyright and is not bundled here.
Processing: tools/make_rodinia_texture.py reconstructs
RodiniaBlocks_WithPlateIDColumnAndIDs.shp with
RodiniaModel_CompleteRotationFile.rot using pyGPlates 1.0 at exactly 1000 Ma,
anchor plate 0; 38 reconstructed geometries. Great-circle edges are tessellated
at 0.2 degrees and wrapped at the map edges. Flat colours and fine block outlines
are added by DEEPscope. The map spans 60 W to 300 E, centred on 120 E in the model
frame, with north up. At zero viewer Texture longitude, +x is this map centre.
This is one published tectonic reconstruction, not a photograph or a unique
solution for Rodinia. Outlines represent continental blocks, not dated shorelines;
the blue background is unrepresented area, not a reconstructed bathymetry.
There is no inferred vegetation, relief, climate or magnetic-field alignment.
To reproduce after unpacking the archive (requires pygplates, matplotlib, Pillow):
python3 tools/make_rodinia_texture.py --model-dir /path/to/Li_etal_2008_RodiniaModel --out public/assets/surfaces/rodinia-1000ma.png

EARLY EARTH, ARTISTIC LAVA SURFACE -- early-earth.png -- 2048 x 1024
Credit: Created using Lava004 from ambientCG.com (Lennart Demes), licensed under
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal. Spherical adaptation: DEEPscope.
Source: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Lava004
Original asset released 12 December 2019; source lists its technique as procedural.
Download: https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Lava004_1K-JPG.zip
Selected file: Lava004_1K-JPG_Color.jpg (1024 x 1024).
Creator licence statement: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Both source material and DEEPscope's adapted image are provided under CC0 1.0.
Processing: tools/wrap_surface_texture.py samples the downloaded colour material
on three perpendicular planes, blends by the eighth power of the absolute sphere
normal, and writes a 2:1 equirectangular PNG. It uses bilinear interpolation and
matching seam/pole samples. No generative AI is used in the bundled adaptation.
This is a decorative lava interpretation for an early molten-Earth episode;
ambientCG did not create a dated map of Earth. No ancient geography, magma
temperature, topography or age is inferred from its colour or pattern. It does
not represent the entire Hadean eon. Normal and emission maps are not applied.
To reproduce after unpacking the archive (requires NumPy and Pillow):
python3 tools/wrap_surface_texture.py --source /path/to/Lava004_1K-JPG_Color.jpg --out public/assets/surfaces/early-earth.png
