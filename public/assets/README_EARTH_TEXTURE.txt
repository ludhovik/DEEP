Bundled Earth texture: earth_blue_marble.png

Unmodified 2048 x 1024 global latitude-longitude (equirectangular) map:
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a002900/a002915/bluemarble-2048.png
Source and credits: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/

Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
Blue Marble data courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA's Earth Observatory.
The image is a reference mosaic, not a live depiction of seasonal snow or sea ice.

To use a different image, replace this PNG with a complete 2:1 equirectangular
map, preferably 4096 x 2048 for closer views. It must span longitude -180 to
+180 degrees and latitude +90 at the top to -90 at the bottom, with Greenwich
at the horizontal centre. Include both poles, with no border, labels or grid.
A Mercator map or a map cropped before the poles cannot be wrapped correctly
by resizing it. Use a genuine global equirectangular source instead.

In the viewer, north is +z. At Texture longitude = 0, Greenwich lies along +x
and 90 degrees east along +y. Positive texture longitude moves Greenwich toward
+y. This corrects the previous half-turn offset; adjust old saved longitude
settings by 180 degrees if reproducing their former continent orientation.
